# Statutory Compliance Checklists for Facilities

This document provides statutory compliance document checklists and verification rules used by the `audit-ready` Skill to audit small clinics, schools, and light manufacturing plants.

## 1. Small Clinics and Healthcare Facilities

Small medical clinics and primary health centers must maintain statutory operational licenses, environmental safety authorizations, and clinical staff credentials.

### Mandatory Compliance Inventory
1. **Clinical Establishment Registration**:
   - Authority: State Health Department or Municipal Corporation.
   - Validity Cycle: Typically 1 to 5 years.
   - Verification Rules: Matches facility legal name, address, and approved bed or consultation capacity.
2. **Biomedical Waste Management (BMWM) Authorization**:
   - Authority: State Pollution Control Board.
   - Validity Cycle: 3 to 5 years.
   - Verification Rules: Valid waste pickup agreement with authorized common biomedical waste treatment facility (CBWTF) and monthly barcoded waste handover register.
3. **Fire Safety Certificate / No Objection Certificate (NOC)**:
   - Authority: State Fire and Rescue Services.
   - Validity Cycle: 1 year (annual renewal).
   - Verification Rules: Verifies building height, fire extinguisher recharge tags, and clear exit routes.
4. **Diagnostic Equipment and Radiation Registration (AERB)**:
   - Authority: Atomic Energy Regulatory Board.
   - Validity Cycle: 5 years (for X-ray, OPG, or CT equipment).
   - Verification Rules: Lead shielding certificate, room layout approval, and Radiation Safety Officer (RSO) appointment.
5. **Pharmacy and Drug Sale License**:
   - Authority: State Drug Control Department (Form 20/21).
   - Validity Cycle: 5 years with annual retention fee receipt.
   - Verification Rules: Registered pharmacist name and cold chain refrigeration temperature log.
6. **Staff Credentials and Medical Council Registrations**:
   - Authority: State Medical Council, State Nursing Council.
   - Validity Cycle: Permanent or periodic renewal receipts.
   - Verification Rules: Active registration certificates for all practicing physicians and registered nurses.
7. **Mandatory Registers and Logs**:
   - Daily Outpatient Department (OPD) register.
   - Staff Hepatitis B and Tetanus vaccination record.
   - Autoclave sterilization temperature and spore test logbook.

## 2. Schools and Educational Institutions

Schools must provide physical infrastructure safety, student protection certifications, and regulatory recognition.

### Mandatory Compliance Inventory
1. **Building Safety and Structural Stability Certificate**:
   - Authority: Public Works Department (PWD) or certified structural engineer.
   - Validity Cycle: 3 years.
   - Verification Rules: Verifies load-bearing stability, disaster resistance, and classroom capacity.
2. **Fire Safety Certificate / NOC**:
   - Authority: Fire Department.
   - Validity Cycle: 1 year.
   - Verification Rules: Working hydrant systems, serviced fire extinguishers, and conducted evacuation drills.
3. **Safe Drinking Water and Sanitary Condition Certificate**:
   - Authority: District Public Health Laboratory or Municipal Health Officer.
   - Validity Cycle: 1 year.
   - Verification Rules: Bacteriological and chemical water purity test report and operational student washrooms.
4. **School Recognition and Affiliation Approval**:
   - Authority: Department of School Education or Central / State Board.
   - Validity Cycle: Multi-year or permanent recognition order.
   - Verification Rules: Current affiliation code and compliance with student-teacher ratios.
5. **Student Transport Safety and Vehicle Fitness**:
   - Authority: Regional Transport Office (RTO).
   - Validity Cycle: Annual vehicle fitness, insurance, and speed governor certification.
   - Verification Rules: Driver commercial badge, driver police verification, and bus CCTV installation.
6. **Child Protection and Mandatory Staff Records**:
   - Police background verification certificates for all teaching and non-teaching personnel.
   - Protection of Children from Sexual Offences (POCSO) Internal Complaints Committee constitution.
   - First aid equipment and student annual health inspection register.

## 3. Light Manufacturing Plants and Factories

Factories must comply with occupational health, environmental protection, and industrial safety regulations.

### Mandatory Compliance Inventory
1. **Factory Operating License**:
   - Authority: Directorate of Industrial Safety and Health (DISH) or Chief Inspector of Factories.
   - Validity Cycle: 1 to 5 years.
   - Verification Rules: Approved worker headcount, sanctioned horsepower/electrical load, and factory layout plan approval.
2. **Consent to Operate (CTO) - Air and Water**:
   - Authority: State Pollution Control Board (under Air and Water Acts).
   - Validity Cycle: 1 to 5 years depending on industrial category (Red/Orange/Green).
   - Verification Rules: Effluent Treatment Plant (ETP) or Sewage Treatment Plant (STP) discharge logs and stack emission reports.
3. **Industrial Fire Safety NOC**:
   - Authority: Fire Safety Directorate.
   - Validity Cycle: 1 year.
   - Verification Rules: Dedicated fire water storage, functional smoke detectors, and trained emergency response squad.
4. **Pressure Vessel and Lifting Machinery Inspection Certificate**:
   - Authority: Competent person recognized by DISH (Form 8 and Form 11).
   - Validity Cycle: 6 months (lifting gear) to 12 months (air receivers, boilers).
   - Verification Rules: Hydrostatic pressure test reports and safe working load (SWL) calibration markings.
5. **Hazardous Waste and Chemical Management Authorization**:
   - Authority: Pollution Control Board.
   - Validity Cycle: 5 years.
   - Verification Rules: Hazardous waste manifest (Form 10) and on-site chemical Material Safety Data Sheets (MSDS).
6. **Social Security and Statutory Labor Registers**:
   - Employees State Insurance (ESIC) and Employees Provident Fund (EPF) monthly contribution returns.
   - Adult worker register (Form 12) and overtime register.
   - Occupational accident and dangerous occurrences register.
   - First aid station replenishment log and certified first aider appointment.

## 4. Inspection Readiness Grading Matrix

The Skill calculates an objective Readiness Score (0 to 100%) based on the following criteria:

- **Inspector-Ready (Score >= 90%)**: All statutory operational licenses and fire NOCs exist and are active with more than 30 days remaining; mandatory registers are present and updated.
- **Conditional Pass (Score 70% to 89%)**: Primary licenses are valid, but secondary records or upcoming expirations within 30 days require immediate renewal notice.
- **Critical Failure (Score < 70%)**: Any primary operational license or fire certificate is expired or completely missing; immediate regulatory exposure or facility shutdown risk.
