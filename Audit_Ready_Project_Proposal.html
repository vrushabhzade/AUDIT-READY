1 What does your Skill do? (one sentence)
Audit Ready autonomously scans, verifies, and organizes statutory compliance documents—such as operating licenses, fire safety certificates, and mandatory registers—on UGREEN AI NAS, compiling an inspector-ready directory and actionable gap report for small facilities.
2 Who is your target user, and what is their problem?
Target users are administrators of small clinics, schools, and light manufacturing plants. They face sudden regulatory audits with scattered paperwork across shared folders, paper binders, and digital scans, leading to missed expiry renewals, heavy fines, or operational shutdown.
3 What is your solution?
Audit Ready delivers an automated, on-premise compliance auditing system. It discovers compliance files, extracts validity dates via local multimodal AI, flags expired or missing statutory items against sector checklists, tags source files, and stages organized documents into an inspector-ready audit folder.
4 What is the main user flow?
1) User specifies facility type (clinic/school/factory) and document directory; 2) Skill scans files and extracts metadata; 3) Skill detects gaps against statutory criteria; 4) User reviews missing/expiring items; 5) Skill stages verified files and outputs a gap report.
5 How is your idea different from existing tools or common AI solutions?
Existing enterprise GRC software is expensive, cloud-hosted, and complex for small businesses. Cloud AI exposes confidential patient, student, and factory records. Audit Ready runs 100% locally on UGREEN AI NAS, guaranteeing absolute privacy, zero SaaS subscription costs, and direct NAS file system reorganization.
6 Which UGREEN AI NAS features does your Skill use, and at which step?
1) ug-filesystem (find/ls/mkdir/cpmv) for file discovery, staging, and folder creation; 2) ug-document (metadata/extract/qa/writing) for PDF text extraction, clause validation, and report drafting; 3) ug-photo (vision-ocr/vision-vlm) for scanned framed certificates and logbook photos; 4) ug-filemgr (tag-add-file) for metadata tagging; 5) ug-knowledge (save-text) for persistent gap report archival.
7 What is your technical approach?
Audit Ready orchestrates specialized NAS agents using Uliya standards. The parent Skill delegates discovery to file-agent, document parsing to document-agent, and visual certificate OCR to photo-agent. It executes on local quantized models (VLM/LLM) without cloud leaks, operating with standard read/write permissions on designated audit shares.
8 What have you built so far? What is not built yet? What still needs testing on a NAS?
Built: Uliya-compliant Skill package (SKILL.md, references), orchestration workflows, status handling, and sector compliance matrices. Not built: interactive web dashboard. Needs testing: hardware VLM extraction latency on multi-page scanned PDF registers and write-permission handling across multi-user NAS shared directories.
9 How will you test your Skill if you become a Finalist? Name two core test cases and one edge case.
Core 1: 50-document clinic dataset with valid/expired medical licenses and fire certificates. Core 2: 30-document factory dataset with mixed scans, equipment calibration logs, and pollution consents. Edge case: Illegible low-light photo of a framed certificate with an ambiguous blurred expiration date triggering user clarification.
10 What are the known limits, risks, or outside dependencies of your Skill?
Limits: Handwritten cursive logbooks may require human verification; non-standard local government certificates require customizable prompt schemas. Risks: Corrupted PDF containers. Dependencies: UGREEN AI NAS local VLM/OCR runtime and ug-document/ug-photo CLI services; zero external internet or cloud API dependencies.
